rationale
Every figure of an apixaban study shows the fitted plasma, urine, feces and clotting times over their data, so the description is read together with the data it describes (influence low); it fixes no regimen of apixaban, and each context of a regimen, a population or an interaction stands on validation studies of its own (consequence low). The PK fit describes 82 % of its training points within 2-fold (GMFE 1.56) with a bias: the plasma after an oral dose is underpredicted (80 % within 2-fold, AFE 0.70; Song2015 0.42, Wang2016 0.49, Shaikh2021 0.53), after an intravenous dose it is not (AFE 0.97), consistent with the onset of the absorption and the late phase of the oral profile (TODO.md P1); the urine and the feces have a GMFE of at most 1.5. The PD fit describes 94 % within 2-fold (GMFE 1.22): the INR, PT, mPT and aPTT with a GMFE of at most 1.07, the relative change of the INR worst (74 %, AFE 0.75), and the anti-factor Xa activity in IU/ml is underpredicted (81 %, AFE 0.58; its fitted slope is below the slope of the data, TODO.md P2, Pharmacodynamics). The acceptance is met (86 % within 2-fold), yet the COU is `insufficient_evidence`: two of its quantities have no calibration point inside the claimed domain, the PT, fitted on Wang2016 alone, whose fasting is not reported, and the relative change of the mPT, fitted on Frost2018 alone, whose application form is not reported (its relative changes after 2.5 mg are outliers). Both studies are NR in the coverage, outside the claimed fasted state and forms, so the PT and the mPT change are uncovered quantities, although every claimed value of the metadata is calibrated by the points of the other quantities.

rationale
C2: 2.5 to 25 mg lie inside the fitted 0.5 to 50 mg, and a repeated regimen is the superposition of fitted single doses. The profile of a regimen which no study measured comes from the model, with the nine studied regimens as the check (influence medium); the indication fixes the regimen of the label, so a simulated profile of a healthy adult changes no regimen and serves the design of a trial or of a simulation (consequence low). Kreutz2017 is the one fed regimen: it is kept as validation evidence and not moved to the limitations, the conservative direction, since its points count in the acceptance although the fed state is not claimed. The fed arms of the single dose food studies are limitations instead, because their AUC change against the fasted arm is no process of the model, while the AUC over a dosing interval at steady state does not depend on the rate of the absorption, which is what the fed state of the model changes. The verdict rests on nine regimens of four studies (217 points) and carries the bias of the oral plasma of the training (AFE 0.78 against 0.70): the low doses are unbiased (2.5 and 5 mg twice daily of Frost2013a, AFE 1.01), the higher doses and the troughs are underpredicted, most 10 mg twice daily of Cui2013 (60 % within 2-fold, AFE 0.46), whose troughs are a quarter of the data (TODO.md P1, terminal half-life and late phase).

rationale
C3: the model describes the populations by its physiology, the renal function by the measured creatinine clearance (the filtration of apixaban and the chronic kidney disease profile of its albumin binding) and the body size by the body weight and the height, and the studies are the data of apixaban in these populations. The acceptance is not met (76 % within 2-fold, GMFE 1.82; 219 points of four studies): the plasma is underpredicted in every study, as the oral plasma of the training (AFE 0.70), most in Chang2016 (AFE 0.43), whose normal group has an exposure a third above the other studies (AFE 0.54), and the bias grows with the impairment (mild 0.45, moderate 0.35, severe 0.41), so the model predicts a smaller rise of the exposure than observed; the elderly of Frost2015 (AFE 0.51, the young of the training 0.66) and the body weights of Upreti2013 (0.65 and 0.58, the reference of the training 0.63, the AUC rises more at a low body weight than observed, TODO.md P1, Body weight) carry the bias of their study.
